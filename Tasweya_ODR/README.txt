# Tasweya ODR: Online Small Claims Dispute Resolution Platform

Project:Project 40, Online Small Claims Dispute Resolution
Client: Tasweya Dispute Resolution Center (Counselor Rehab Ismail, Center Director)

## About the project

Many people in Egypt have small disputes (a refused refund, a withheld deposit, an unpaid freelance invoice) that never get resolved, because going to court takes too long and costs more than the amount in dispute.

Tasweya offers a cheaper path for claims below 100,000 EGP: negotiation, then mediation, then arbitration, all online. This project is the software platform for that process. It covers:

- Online case filing with evidence upload, and a scope check that redirects out-of-scope cases to the right court
- Filing fees, with a waiver for legal-aid claimants
- Respondent notification by SMS and email, with proof of delivery
- Negotiation with structured offers and an AI assistant that only assists and never decides
- Mediation (21 days) and arbitration (written decision within 30 days)
- Electronic settlements and signatures
- Escrow payments released when agreed conditions are met
- Reports for the Center (settlement rate by stage, duration by case type, mediator performance, escrow volume)
